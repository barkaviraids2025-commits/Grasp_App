Chapter 1: Percentages and Ratios
A percentage is a number or ratio expressed as a fraction of 100.
To find x% of y, calculate (x * y) / 100.

Chapter 2: Time, Speed and Distance
Speed is distance divided by time (S = D / T).
When trains cross each other in opposite directions, add relative speeds.

Chapter 3: Profit and Loss
Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price. Profit percentage is calculated on Cost Price.
